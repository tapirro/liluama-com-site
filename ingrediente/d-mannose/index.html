Mecanismo de acción

Compuesto activo: D-mannose (monosacárido natural)
Dosis en Liluama: 500 mg

D-mannose is a naturally occurring monosaccharide that provides targeted protection against urinary tract infections through a unique competitive inhibition mechanism. Uropathogenic E. coli (UPEC), responsible for 80-90% of UTIs, use type 1 fimbriae with FimH adhesin to bind to mannose receptors (uroplakin Ia) on the bladder epithelium. Oral D-mannose is absorbed in the small intestine and excreted unchanged in the urine, where it saturates the FimH binding sites on E. coli fimbriae, preventing bacterial attachment to the bladder wall. The unattached bacteria are then flushed out during urination. This mechanism is highly specific to type 1 fimbriated E. coli and does not affect the commensal urinary microbiome or contribute to antibiotic resistance.

Evidencia científica

Kranjčec et al. (2014, World Journal of Urology): D-mannose powder (2g/day for 6 months) was as effective as nitrofurantoin in preventing recurrent UTIs (14.6% vs 20.4% recurrence) with significantly fewer side effects. Porru et al. (2014, Journal of Clinical Urology): D-mannose (1g every 8 hours for 2 weeks, then 1g twice daily) significantly reduced UTI recurrence and improved quality of life. De Nunzio et al. (2017): combination of D-mannose with cranberry extract reduced UTI episodes by 75% versus cranberry alone.
